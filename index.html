What is HEIC? Why convert to JPG?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Format) is the default image format on Apple devices running iOS 11 and later. While it saves storage space, many devices, websites, and Windows applications cannot open or display HEIC files directly. Converting them to JPG ensures universal compatibility.

How is your privacy protected?

Unlike traditional online converters, your images are never uploaded to any remote server or third-party cloud service. All conversion processing is handled entirely within your browser engine on your local computer.
